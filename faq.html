Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? You might find your answer below! Browse through some of our frequently asked questions about orders, shipping, products and more.

DO YOU OFFER CUSTOM ORDERS?

I don’t offer any custom orders, as I need to concentrate on the items that are on my website. I’m sorry!

WHAT IS YOUR RETURN/EXCHANGE POLICY?

I typically do not accept returns or exchanges due to the made-to-order nature of my shop. However, I will refund you or replace the item if it is damaged in the mail or if the item is defective.

Just email me about any issues ( contact@ktscanvases.com), and I'll be happy to help!

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET MY ORDER?

Most items are shipped out in 2–5 days.

DO YOU SHIP INTERNATIONALLY?

I am only able to ship items from my website to the US. However, I do have some prints available through iCanvas that can be shipped internationally.

I also have a Redbubble shop, and my designs are on Casetify. I have some apparel available through Bonfire.

You can explore these additional shops below:
